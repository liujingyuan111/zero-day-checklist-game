# Third-Party Research and License Notes

## First-night graybox

- The small courier billboard combines three tiles from Kenney's [Roguelike Character Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/roguelike-character-pack). Base and clothing are composited once, with nearest filtering in Godot. See `art/first_night/Kenney-characters-license.txt`.
- The one-shot interaction and sense sounds are Kenney's [UI SFX Set](https://opengameart.org/content/51-ui-sound-effects-buttons-switches-and-clicks) (`switch15.wav`) and [Digital SFX Set](https://opengameart.org/content/63-digital-sound-effects-lasers-phasers-space-etc) (`phaserUp4.mp3`). The two author's README files are included beside the clips.
- The first-night buildings, lights, weather palette, route marks and echoes are generated by this project's Godot scripts. No external plug-in is required for the new run.
